CLAUDE REFERENCE PACK — 29 October 2026
Scope: telephone_vowel_81_extracted_v2 and italian_vowel_a_grouped_v1 only.
The schema retains historical profile IDs; use only these two speech profiles.
This reference pack is frozen by the accompanying hashes. It does not establish
that every benchmark implementation or scoring rule has yet been frozen.

Metrics: sensitivity = PD cases detected / all PD cases; specificity = healthy
correct / all healthy. False-positive rate = 1-specificity; false-negative rate
= 1-sensitivity. Balanced accuracy = (sensitivity+specificity)/2.
Maximize accuracy, balanced accuracy, sensitivity, specificity. Minimize false
positive and false negative rates. Thresholds are fractions in [0,1].
Metric constraints use inclusive min/max. Strict inequalities are not expressible
and require clarification. Preserve intended strictness in reference notes.
Each objective, constraint and tie-breaker requires exact supporting request text.
One primary objective, with only explicitly ordered tie-breakers. A threshold
alone leaves ranking unspecified and requires clarification. Ambiguous best/safe
requests require clarification; do not invent an objective or threshold.
CPU support can be required. Deployment limits support explicit milliseconds
and explicit USD per case. Other units/qualitative limits require clarification.
Missing measurements remain unknown. All registered speech latency/cost values
are unmeasured; do not treat them as zero. No registry evidence of hospital
validation or clinical approval is supplied.
Compare models only within trusted profile and its evaluation identifier.
Do not transfer performance evidence across profiles. Requirements are checked
against recorded estimates; they do not guarantee performance on future patients.
A clear unconditional named model choice is distinct from policy recommendation.
Conditional named choices require clarification in the current interface scope.
Explicit choices do not enforce another policy's metric constraints.

OUTCOMES
selected: one eligible best candidate under the stated ranking.
tied: criteria leave multiple equally ranked eligible candidates; no hidden tie-break.
no_eligible_model: known evidence excludes all candidates.
insufficient_deployment_evidence: a potentially eligible candidate lacks requested
deployment measurements; do not silently favor a more measured candidate.
needs_clarification: missing objective, unsupported or ambiguous requirements.
unsupported_profile / no_evaluation_evidence: profile absent or no matching evidence.
Invalid generated policies must be blocked, not executed.
Use a numerical tolerance of 1e-12 for metric comparisons and ranking ties.

Ground truths must be reviewed. Use deterministic computation for numerical
outcomes. Do not inspect implementation prompts/parser grammar or prior outputs.
Do not generate requests to exploit known parser gaps. Model-generated requests
are not independently human-authored; retain actual preparation provenance.
