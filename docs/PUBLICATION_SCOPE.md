Not published: serialized fitted classifiers; participant-linked manifests, training/source identifiers, input hashes and individual predictions; original per-case benchmark response logs and reference/input bundles. Automatic publication review blocked the broader payload. Those records remain in the author release packages. This public copy does not by itself reproduce every result from source data.

Eight real image entry-point checks passed. These are earlier examined splits, not new independent validation cohorts. NewHandPD identity ambiguity remains disclosed. The status runner recorded 13 image control successes and 11 pending speech cases; individual records are not in this public update.

The selector uses point estimates. Confidence-bound eligibility is future work. The 120 policy requests were model-generated and references were implementation-informed.
